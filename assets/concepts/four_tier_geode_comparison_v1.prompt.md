Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: Minecraft mod art direction comparison sheet for four increasingly advanced workstation blocks.
Input images: Image 1 is the approved black-gold piglin conversion table and must be reproduced faithfully as tier 1. They are reference designs, not inspiration for replacing them.
Primary request: Create a single wide, polished side-by-side comparison image with exactly FOUR distinct workstation blocks, in left-to-right order: approved black-gold, approved End, approved Sculk, then a NEW highest-tier budding-amethyst geode proliferation table. All four have the same 16x16x16 Minecraft block footprint, equal apparent scale, identical three-quarter isometric camera angle, identical lighting, clean neutral dark slate background, and are clearly separated. No pedestal height tricks. Their increasing sophistication comes from the silhouettes: tier 1 low recessed core; tier 2 levitating square ring; tier 3 rising sculk crystal columns; tier 4 a structured geode crown of crystal buds.
New fourth block design: polished dark gray smooth-basalt outer foundation and thick chalk-white calcite geometric shell, inspired by a geode cross section. Ivory and pale gray dominate about 75% of its visible area. Inside a recessed central cavity, a precise tiered cluster of translucent-looking pale rose, silver-white and faint lavender amethyst buds grows upward in four rigid voxel stages; subtle pearly highlights. Show one larger central bud and smaller symmetric buds, contained within the block volume. Small exposed amethyst seams in the calcite show growth. The new design should feel precious, mature, slow-growing, and powerful, but still fit vanilla Minecraft block modeling: crisp integer-voxel shapes, native 16x16 pixel-texture language, no smooth bevels, no arbitrary tiny greebles.
Palette separation is essential: retain the End table's saturated purpur purple, black obsidian and yellowish end-stone frame exactly as reference; make the new geode table predominantly white/light gray with only restrained pale pink-lavender crystal accents. Do not use a black obsidian body, purple purpur corner guards, levitating ring, cyan sculk glow, gold piglin clamps, or bone fan on the new block.
Visual style: high-quality neutral studio render of four actual Minecraft voxel block models; sharp pixel edges and readable silhouettes, not a painting, not photoreal stone, not generic fantasy machinery.
Composition: landscape four-column lineup, each block wholly visible with comfortable spacing, front and top surfaces both visible. No captions, labels, arrows, UI, watermark, humans or props.
